Anaadi tells Indian mythology in story order, one short episode a day on YouTube, in Telugu.

1. Art first. Every character gets three sheets (turnaround, expressions, poses). Each new character comes to you here as a "Character approval". Nothing uses a character before you approve it; a rejected one is redrawn from your note.
2. Story and voice. Each episode is written from the series bible with its sources, then recorded.
3. Shots. Start frames are made from the approved sheets, then turned into clips with Gemini video.
4. Build. Clips, voice, Telugu captions and source tags are put together into one 45 to 60 second video.
5. Review. The finished episode arrives here as a video approval, numbered "Ep N".
6. Post. Approved episodes are scheduled on YouTube by themselves, strictly in episode order, one a day at 19:30 IST, and added to the season playlist.

Episodes are built ahead as fast as the free Gemini limits allow; only posting is once a day.
